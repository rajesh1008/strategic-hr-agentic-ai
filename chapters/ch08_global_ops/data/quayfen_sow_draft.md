# Statement of Work No. 7: HR Service Desk 2027 (vendor draft)

SYNTHETIC. Invented for Strategic HR and Agentic AI, Chapter 8.
Between Hartwell Infrastructure & Services ("Client") and
Quayfen Outsourcing ("Supplier"). Draft of March 15, 2027.

## 3. Services

3.1 Supplier shall operate the Client's HR virtual agent
("Virtual Agent") for Employees in the United States, Canada
and the United Kingdom, and shall staff the Adviser
queue that receives Contacts the Virtual Agent transfers.

3.2 Supplier shall operate the Virtual Agent in accordance with
the Client's Automate/Keep Line, version 1.0, attached as
Exhibit D, as the Client may update it from time to time.

3.3 Supplier's Workforce Management team may adjust Virtual
Agent routing, tool configuration and Adviser permissions as
needed to manage peak volumes.

3.4 Supplier shall communicate the resolution of any Service
Incident directly to the affected Employee.

## 4. Service levels

4.1 First response: 90% of Contacts answered within 60 seconds.

4.2 Deflection Rate: the percentage of Contacts closed by the
Virtual Agent without transfer to an Adviser. Target 60%. Each
full point above target earns the Supplier a gain-share credit
under Schedule 3.

4.3 Escalated Contacts are handled within the Service Levels in
section 4.1.

## 5. Commercials

5.1 Based on Supplier's benchmark containment of 60% across
comparable clients, Supplier commits to reduce the dedicated
Hartwell seat count by 189 seats no later than September 30,
2027, with the corresponding reduction in charges.

## 6. Incidents and data

6.1 Supplier shall notify Client of any Service Incident within
seventy-two (72) hours of becoming aware of it.

6.2 Supplier may engage the subcontractors listed in Schedule 5
(Cebu facilities services; background screening partner).

6.3 Advisers in the Philippines may view United Kingdom Employee
records in HartwellOne. Viewing does not constitute a transfer
of data and no additional transfer terms apply.
