Hartwell screening guidance v1 (synthetic)
Role: Field Service Technician (HVAC and building systems)
Posted requirements:
1. At least one EPA Section 608 certification of any type
   (Type I, II, III or Universal), however it is written.
   NATE, OSHA, state licenses, Canadian ODP cards, industry
   safety courses and certifications not yet passed do not
   count.
2. At least 24 months of field service. Field service is
   hands-on installation, maintenance or repair of HVAC,
   refrigeration, electrical or building systems, including
   apprenticeships and military HVAC/R maintenance. Parts
   counter, dispatch, sales, warehouse and customer-service
   roles do not count.
Counting months: months in a role = end month minus start
month (Jan 2025 to Jan 2026 is 12). "Present" means May 2027.
Where two field roles overlap, count the overlapping months
once.
An applicant meets the posted requirements only if both
1 and 2 hold. Nothing else is considered.
